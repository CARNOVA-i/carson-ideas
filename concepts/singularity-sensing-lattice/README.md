# 3D Singularity Sensing Lattice

Status: 💡 Concept

This project investigates whether a coherent wave field containing multiple engineered phase singularities could act as a distributed sensing lattice. The central idea is that perturbations to the field may be inferred from correlated changes in the positions, trajectories, or topology of many singularities across a volume.

This is currently theoretical. Phase singularities are zero-amplitude points or lines where phase becomes undefined. Apparent superluminal motion of interference features does not imply faster-than-light information transfer.

Possible application areas may include RF sensing, precision metrology, strain sensing, environmental sensing, and fundamental physics. No performance claims have yet been validated.
