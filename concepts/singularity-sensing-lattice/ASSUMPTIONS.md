# Assumptions

The following are hypotheses and assumptions requiring validation. They are not established conclusions:

- coherent or sufficiently phase-stable sources are available
- the underlying complex field can be measured or reconstructed
- phase singularities can be localized with useful precision
- perturbations produce measurable changes in the singularity structure
- useful signal can be separated from environmental and instrumental noise
- collective singularity behavior provides information beyond conventional field measurements
